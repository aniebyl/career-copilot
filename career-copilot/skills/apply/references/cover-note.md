# Cover note

A cover note is 120–180 words, not a formal letter. Its only job: connect *this* person to
*this* role, fast, in a human voice.

## Structure

1. **Hook (1 line).** Why you're writing now — a referral name, a specific thing the company
   is doing, or a sharp statement of fit. ("[Mutual] suggested I reach out…" / "You're scaling
   LLM eval — that's the last two years of my work.")
2. **Proof (2–3 lines).** The single most relevant accomplishment, quantified, mapped to the
   role's top theme. Not a resume recap — the one thing that matters here.
3. **Why them (1–2 lines).** Something specific about the company/team — shows you did the
   work, not a mail-merge.
4. **Close (1 line).** A low-friction next step ("Happy to share how I'd approach X.").

## Do / Don't

- **Do** use the JD's own language for the role and themes; lead with impact; keep it
  skimmable.
- **Don't** restate the whole resume, use clichés ("proven track record"), over-flatter, or
  exceed ~180 words.
- **Match the user's voice** — if `profile/voice.md` exists, follow it; otherwise mirror the
  tone of their resume and confirm once.

## Truthfulness

Every claim must be backed by the tailored resume. If the role needs something the user
lacks, address it honestly or don't claim it.
