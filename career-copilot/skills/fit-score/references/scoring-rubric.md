# Scoring rubric

## Gate first (hard must-haves)

Before weighting, check the profile's hard must-haves (work authorization / sponsorship,
remote-only, minimum base, non-negotiable location, etc.). **If any hard must-have fails, cap the
score at ≤ 30 and tier C**, and state which gate failed. Don't let a high weighted score override
a failed gate.

## Weighted axes (applied only after gates pass)

| Axis | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role / title match | 25 | How close to `targets.roles` |
| Seniority match | 15 | Right level — under- or over-leveled both hurt |
| Industry / domain match | 15 | Against `targets.industries` |
| Experience fit | 20 | Resume/brief vs the role's must-haves |
| Location / remote | 10 | Against `targets.locations` + preference |
| Comp fit | 10 | Against `targets.comp_target` |
| Signal / freshness | 5 | Real, current posting; strong hiring signal |

Sum to a 0–100 score.

## Tiers

- **A (75–100)** — strong fit; pursue now.
- **B (50–74)** — possible; pursue if capacity, or after A's.
- **C (< 50, or a failed gate)** — skip or snooze; say why.

## Output per role

`Role · score · tier · one-line why · biggest gap/risk`. Keep scores whole numbers. Recommend the
top few A/B roles to `research` and `tailor-resume` next.
