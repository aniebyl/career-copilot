# STAR method + question categories

## STAR — the structure for behavioral answers

- **Situation** (1 line) — the context.
- **Task** (1 line) — your specific responsibility.
- **Action** (2–3 lines) — what *you* did (use "I", not "we").
- **Result** (1 line) — the outcome, quantified where true.

Keep each answer ~45–60 seconds spoken. One strong, true story beats three vague ones.

## Question categories to prepare

- **Behavioral** — leadership, conflict, failure, ambiguity, influence, prioritization. Map each
  to the role's core competencies.
- **Role / technical** — pull straight from the `jd_spec` must-haves; prepare a concrete example
  for each.
- **Company-specific** — why this company (specific, not generic), reaction to their mission /
  recent news, how you'd approach a problem they actually have.
- **The classics** — why this role, biggest weakness (real + how you manage it), where you want
  to grow.
- **Questions for them** — 3–5 sharp ones about the team, the role's first 90 days, success
  metrics, and the challenge they're hiring to solve.

## Honesty rules

- Build every answer from the user's real experience and `profile.md`.
- If there's no true story for a competency, flag it and prep the closest adjacent example — never
  invent one.
