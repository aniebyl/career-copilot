# Intel brief template

Fill every section. Keep it tight — this is a working brief, not an essay. Cite sources
inline as `[n]`.

---

## TL;DR
- **Fit:** Strong / Possible / Stretch — one sentence why.
- **Best angle:** the single strongest thing to lead with.
- **Top referral path:** name → relationship → why they'd help (or "none found yet").

## Company snapshot
What they build · stage · last round + investors · headcount · traction/customers · HQ /
remote. 4–6 lines max. `[n]`

## Why they're hiring (signals)
The evidence this role is real and funded: recent raise, headcount growth, leadership
hires, roadmap statements, repeated themes in the JD. `[n]`

## Role decoded
- **Must-haves:** …
- **Nice-to-haves:** …
- **ATS keywords / exact skill terms:** …
- **Seniority cues:** scope, team size, reports-to, comp band if inferable.
- **The team's real problem:** what they're actually trying to solve by hiring this.

## People
- **Likely hiring manager:** name/title (or best inference) `[n]`
- **AI/ML leaders nearby:** …
- **Possible referrers:** name → why they're a path (handed to `refer`).

## Fit angle
- **Lead with:** 2–3 strengths mapped directly to must-haves.
- **Gaps + how to address:** the 1–2 real gaps and the honest framing for each.

## Smart questions to ask
3–5 questions that show you've done the work and surface fit/risk.

## Risks / flags
Stage risk, churn signals, vague JD, title inflation, anything that should temper interest.

2. …
