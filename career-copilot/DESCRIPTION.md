# Career Copilot — your AI partner for the entire job search

## Short version (for the marketplace listing / `marketplace.json` description)

Career Copilot is an AI agent for your whole job search: it scouts roles, researches companies,
tailors your resume to each job (truthfully), maps warm referral paths through people you
already know, drafts your applications and outreach in your voice, and learns what works for you
over time. It drafts; you send.

## Full description

Most tools help with one slice: a resume tweak here, a job alert there. Career Copilot runs the
*whole* pipeline with you — it finds the right roles, researches the companies, tailors your
resume to each job, maps the warm path in through people you actually know, drafts your
applications and outreach, and gets smarter about what works for you every week. Everything it
writes is truthful, and nothing goes out without you: it drafts, you send.

### How it helps you, end to end

- **Find roles worth your time.** It scouts job boards and the open web for openings that fit
  your target, filters the noise, and drops fresh matches into one pipeline — so you stop
  tab-hoarding and start focusing.
- **Walk in informed.** For any role you get a tight intel brief: what the company does, why
  they're hiring, who the likely hiring manager is, the must-haves decoded from the posting, and
  an honest read on your fit.
- **Nail the job/resume fit.** Paste a job description and it rewrites your resume to match —
  truthfully reordering and relabeling your real experience and weaving in the exact keywords
  that clear automated screens. You get a clean, ATS-safe resume *and* a fit report that shows
  precisely what changed and flags any real gaps, so you know which jobs are genuinely worth
  applying to. It never invents experience you don't have; when something isn't a fit, it says so.
- **Get referred, not buried.** A cold application lands in a stack of hundreds; a referral skips
  the line. Career Copilot finds who you already know at a target company — and who could
  introduce you — ranks the warmest paths, and drafts the intro ask. It keeps your search
  discreet, routing only through people you're comfortable knowing you're looking.
- **Apply and reach out — in your voice.** It drafts tailored applications, cover notes, "why
  this company" answers, referral asks, and follow-ups that sound like you, ready to review and
  send. Drafts only, always.
- **Never drop a thread.** One live dashboard tracks every role and contact, shows what needs
  action today, and nudges you to follow up at the right moment — without ever over-nudging.
- **Get better every week.** It quietly records what happens — which messages earn replies, which
  applications advance — and adapts your strategy, so your search compounds instead of repeating.

### Why it's different

- **Truthful by design** — it sharpens how you present real experience; it never fabricates.
- **You're in control** — it drafts; you decide and send.
- **It compounds** — the more you use it, the better it knows your voice, your targets, and
  what's working.

Built for anyone running a job search — across roles, industries, and levels.
